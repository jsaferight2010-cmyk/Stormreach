## Welcome to the storm

Stormreach is a storm-swept coast where everything is connected. Storms grow crystals, crystals become gear, and gear calls more storms. The further you go, the bigger the weather gets: tornadoes, hail, a spreading Stormblight, a whole sky dimension and an old story waiting at the end of it.

This book explains how it all works. For recipes, open the **Storm Crafter's Handbook**. Every item also explains itself: hover over it, and hold **Shift** for the details.

- Start on the **Stormreach Coast**, a grey, windswept shore that replaces some cool, wet beaches.
- Wait for rain. Everything in the mod grows from the weather.

## Your first storm

When it rains, the coast turns into a **Gale**. Gusts push you around outside, so sneak to brace yourself. When it thunders, it becomes a **Thunderhead**, with stronger wind, lightning close by and hostile **Squalls** forming in the clouds.

- **Lightning on Stormstone** can turn it into **Storm Crystal Ore**. That's how crystals come back, so the coast never runs out.
- **Squalls** ram you and knock you back, and they drop **Storm Shards**. They blow apart when the storm ends.
- A **Storm Barometer** gives a redstone signal for the weather (0 clear, 7 rain, 15 thunder). Use it to open its dial and watch the pressure and trend.
- Look for **Cliff Crabs** (breed them with Stormberries) and **Storm Gulls** (breed them with fish) along the shore.

## Storm gear

Storm Shards and Storm Crystals become your first real equipment.

- **Stormshard tools and armour** are a clear step past iron. A full set of the armour makes you immune to lightning.
- **Storm Surge:** Stormreach weapons and tools work harder in bad weather. You get more damage and faster mining in rain, and even more in a thunderstorm.
- The **Stormshard sword** can call lightning down on what you hit during a thunderstorm.
- The **Tempest (Lightning) Staff** strikes lightning where you aim. Its bolts never set anything on fire, and while you hold it no lightning can set you alight.
- The **Thunderhead Horn** summons a thunderstorm when you need one.
- Later tiers come from the Tempest Realm: **Voltite** (as good as diamond), **Aurorite** (a little past netherite) and finally **Maelstrom** gear.

## Reading the weather

Weather instruments tell you what's coming. Use any of them to open its screen.

- **Barometer:** a dial with a needle, a brass "set" hand and a trend. Falling pressure means worse weather.
- **Wind Vane:** a vane that swings to point into the wind.
- **Anemometer** and **Thermometer:** wind speed and gusts, temperature, humidity and dew point.
- **Weather Station** and **Doppler Radar:** the full picture, including rain and rotation on the radar. The radar and other tall machines show a see-through preview of the whole structure before you place them.
- **Weather Radio:** broadcasts warnings, including when a tornado is on the ground near you.
- **Storm Chaser goggles** show the weather on your screen.
- **Units:** imperial by default, metric in the settings. On a server, everyone sees their own units.
- **Weatherglass:** a storm glass that grows crystals as rain approaches, up to 20 minutes before it arrives. Clear means settled; a few crystals, rain on the way; cloudy, rain; full, a thunderstorm. A comparator reads it from 0 to 15.

## Signs of the storm

- **Freezing rain:** where it is only just above freezing (taiga, stony shores, windswept hills), rain freezes as it lands at night and in Thunderheads. It leaves **Glaze**, a skin of clear ice as slippery as ice, and you feel the cold. Glaze melts by day, or at once near a torch.
- **St. Elmo's fire:** in a Thunderhead, holding a metal tool or weapon out in the open above y 90 makes it glow and hiss. After 10 seconds you are warned; after 30, it draws lightning down on you. Get under cover, get lower, put it away, or stand within 16 blocks of a lightning rod, which takes the charge.
- **Storms feed the fields:** crops open to the rain grow about twice as fast while it rains, and lightning fixes nitrogen: crops within 4 blocks of a strike get a burst of growth, as if bone-mealed.

## Tornadoes

Most tornadoes come out of supercell thunderstorms. They range from weak, thin ropes to huge wedges wrapped in rain, rated on the **EF scale** (EF0 to EF5) by the damage they do.

- **Stay safe:** listen for warnings, get underground or behind thick walls, and use a **Storm Shelter Hatch**. Debris is dangerous, so don't stand in the open.
- Storms also bring **hail** and **downbursts**. Hail is blocked by any roof.
- **Chasing:** drive the **Tornado Intercept Vehicle** (press **Z** to drop its anchor spikes), place **Tornado Probes** in a tornado's path, launch **Weather Balloons**, and record it all in the **Storm Chaser's Logbook**.
- **Dispel Weather** (Creative) ends the storms and weather events around you.

## Storm power

Storms make power, measured in **SU**.

- The **Wind Turbine** is a tall three-bladed machine (it needs a clear column 16 blocks high) that turns into the wind.
- **Storm Capacitors** store lightning, **Copper Cable** carries power, and a **Multimeter** shows what's flowing.
- Power runs **Arc Lamps**, **Shock Plates**, **Electric Pumps**, **Sprinklers** and **Tesla Coils**.
- **Rain Barrels** and **Cisterns** collect water from the sky.

## Backpacks

Craft a **Diamond Backpack**, then upgrade it a tier at a time with **Upgrade Kits**. Upgrading keeps the contents, enchantments and name.

- Wear it in its own slot in your inventory, or carry it.
- Press **B** (rebindable) to open it, even with your inventory open.
- Bigger packs have pages. **Sort** tidies them.
- Backpack enchantments (Capacity, Magnetism, Organization, Resilience, Soulbound) only go on backpacks.

## The Stormblight

A **Stormheart** spreads the storm. Wake it with a Storm Cell, lightning or redstone, and it turns the land and mobs around it into storm versions, out to a set radius.

- Water becomes **Stormwater** and lava becomes **Stormlava**.
- **Salt** calms a Stormheart.
- **Overcharge:** once a heart is fully grown, feed it storm power (a Storm Capacitor or cable touching it) and it keeps spreading with no limit. Cut the power and it stops where it is.

## The Tempest Realm

A sky dimension of floating islands, clouds you can fall into, Galewood forests and storm creatures. To get there:

1. Find a **Tempest Spire** in the Stormreach biomes and climb the spiral stair inside to the **Sovereign Altar** on its crown.
2. Wake the **Tempest Sovereign**, either in a thunderstorm or by using a **Tempest Sigil** on the altar. The fight takes place in a sky arena that's built only for the fight.
3. When it falls you're brought back down, and its **Tempest Eye** floats over the altar. Use the Eye to take it. You need four.
4. Build a flat 5×5 ring of 16 **Stormgate Frames** around a 3×3 pool of Stormwater, and set the Eyes in the frames.
5. Strike the pool with lightning and step through the **Stormgate**.

In Creative, the **Creative Realm Pass** takes you there and back instantly.

## The Hollow Labyrinth

A huge maze buried deep in the deepslate. There's no way down from the surface: you have to find it.

- **Three Labyrinth Keys** wait in vaults in its corners. Bring them to the **Keyhole** to open the sealed treasure chamber in the centre.
- The **Hollow Keeper** guards the maze. It hunts by sight and by sound.
- **Hide in a Rusted Locker** to escape it. Don't move while you're inside.

## The story (spoilers)

Someone is following you. You may notice a figure at the edge of your vision that looks like you, and it's gone when you look straight at it. Torn journal pages are hidden around the world. Gather them, along with a **Hollow Remnant** left where the figure stood, to rebind the **Weathered Journal**.

- The **Hollow Shrine** floats high in the Tempest Realm sky. Its ritual has three stages:
  - place a relic on each of the four pedestals (right-click them for the riddles)
  - strike the four Storm Conduits with lightning in the order of their notches
  - lay the journal on the Hollow Altar
- What answers is told in a short cinematic. Press **Jump** to skip it. The fight then moves to a sky arena over the shrine.

## Settings

- **Mod Menu** holds the performance settings (tornadoes, effects and load), each with a tooltip.
- **Gameplay options** are in `config/stormreach.properties`.
- Commands: anyone can use `/stormreach forecast` and `/stormreach status`; operators also have `/stormreach thunderhead`, `/stormreach calm` and `/stormreach reload`.
- On a server, the server's own `stormreach.properties` decides everything but the visual settings.
